Getting this onto the car
  1. Download the PNG (it is already the template's size and kept under 1 MB).
  2. Mobile app (v4.59.0+): Creations → Wrap → Upload, or copy the files into a folder named Wraps at the root of a USB drive (exFAT / FAT32 / MS-DOS FAT / ext3 / ext4 — not NTFS).
  3. In the car: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps.
  4. Limits: PNG, 512×512 to 1024×1024, 1 MB max, name of up to 30 letters, numbers, spaces, dashes or underscores, 10 wraps from the app plus 10 from USB.

White areas of the template are the paintable skin; the black lines are panel edges and are normally left out of the finished file.
